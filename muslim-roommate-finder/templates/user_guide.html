How to Use the App

For Room Seekers

1. Complete Your Profile

Go to Dashboard → Edit Profile. Fill in personal info, Islamic preferences, and upload a photo.

2. Search for Rooms

Use homepage search bar or click "Advanced Filters" for detailed options.

3. Contact Room Owners

Click "Contact" on listings to send messages and introduce yourself.

4. Manage Your Search

Save favorites, check inbox, and update profile as needed.

For Room Owners

1. List Your Room

Go to Dashboard → "List a Room". Fill in details, pricing, and upload photos.

2. Manage Your Listings

View all listings in "My Listings", edit details, and respond to inquiries.

3. Communicate

Check inbox for messages, respond promptly, and ask lifestyle questions.

Islamic Features

Halal-Friendly Filtering

  • Zabihah-only: Filter for users who only eat halal meat
  • Prayer-friendly: Find roommates who respect prayer times
  • Guest policies: Set preferences for visitors in shared spaces

Gender-Based Filtering

  • Automatic filtering: Men only see male roommates/rooms
  • Privacy protection: Ensures Islamic guidelines are followed
  • Separate listings: Room listings are gender-separated

Cultural Considerations

  • Shared values: Connect with fellow Muslims
  • Lifestyle compatibility: Find roommates with similar practices
  • Respectful environment: Built-in Islamic principles
