## 🏠 Core Features

### **1. Browse Available Rooms**
- **View room listings** with photos, descriptions, and amenities
- **Filter by location, price, and Islamic preferences**
- **Contact room owners** directly through the messaging system
- **Save favorites** for later review

### **2. Create Your Profile**
- **Personal information**: Name, age, gender, city
- **Islamic preferences**: Zabihah-only, prayer-friendly, guests policy
- **Lifestyle details**: Bio, contact preferences
- **Profile photo** to help others recognize you

### **3. List Your Room**
- **Room details**: Type, price, availability date
- **Amenities**: What's included (WiFi, parking, etc.)
- **Islamic considerations**: Halal-friendly policies
- **Multiple photos** to showcase your space

### **4. Advanced Search & Filtering**
- **Location-based search** (city filtering)
- **Price range** filtering
- **Gender-based filtering** (men see male listings, women see female)
- **Islamic preference** filtering
- **Age range** filtering

## 🎯 How to Use the App

### **For Room Seekers**

1. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Go to Dashboard → Edit Profile
   - Fill in your personal information
   - Set your Islamic preferences
   - Upload a profile photo

2. **Search for Rooms**
   - Use the homepage search bar
   - Click "Advanced Filters" for detailed options
   - Browse through available rooms
   - Click on rooms for detailed information

3. **Contact Room Owners**
   - Click "Contact" on any room listing
   - Send a message introducing yourself
   - Mention your Islamic preferences and lifestyle

4. **Manage Your Search**
   - Save favorite rooms for later
   - Check your messages in the inbox
   - Update your profile as needed

### **For Room Owners**

1. **List Your Room**
   - Go to Dashboard → "List a Room"
   - Fill in room details and pricing
   - Upload multiple photos
   - Set Islamic preferences and policies

2. **Manage Your Listings**
   - View all your listings in "My Listings"
   - Edit room details as needed
   - Respond to interested users
   - Mark rooms as unavailable when rented

3. **Communicate with Potential Roommates**
   - Check your inbox for messages
   - Respond to inquiries promptly
   - Ask questions about their lifestyle and preferences

## 🕌 Islamic Features

### **Halal-Friendly Filtering**
- **Zabihah-only**: Filter for users who only eat halal meat
- **Prayer-friendly**: Find roommates who respect prayer times
- **Guest policies**: Set preferences for visitors in shared spaces

### **Gender-Based Filtering**
- **Automatic filtering**: Men only see male roommates/rooms, women only see female
- **Privacy protection**: Ensures Islamic guidelines are followed
- **Separate listings**: Room listings are also gender-separated

### **Cultural Considerations**
- **Shared values**: Connect with fellow Muslims
- **Lifestyle compatibility**: Find roommates with similar Islamic practices
- **Respectful environment**: Built-in features to maintain Islamic principles

### **Common Issues & Solutions**

- Try refreshing the page (Ctrl+F5)
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different browser

**Can't login?**
- Verify username and password are correct
- Try the test accounts listed above
- Clear browser cache and cookies

**Advanced filters not working?**
- Make sure JavaScript is enabled
- Try clicking the filter button multiple times
- Refresh the page if needed
